# Super Panavision 70

A large-format process of the late 1950s and 1960s: spherical lenses on 65 mm negative running five perforations a frame, a picture 52.48 by 23.01 mm, nearly three and a half times the area of a 35 mm frame. Release prints were 70 mm, with room for six magnetic sound tracks. Lawrence of Arabia and 2001: A Space Odyssey were shot this way.

This mode exposes Eastman Color Negative 5251 on 65 mm, develops it in the period's ECN-1, prints and projects it. The large frame means the grain and softness of the film are small against the picture.
