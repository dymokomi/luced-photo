# Television: negative to tape

From the 1950s into the 2000s most American television drama and comedy was shot on 35 mm negative and never printed. The negative was developed, then run through a telecine, a scanner that turned it straight into video while a colorist set its color scene by scene.

This mode is that chain: the camera's light, the film and its development, the scan, the colorist's grade, a display rendering, and the framing. It is the default because it is also how film is mostly seen today: scanned.
