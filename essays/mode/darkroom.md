# Darkroom

The black-and-white photographer's chain: the camera, a color filter on the lens if the sky needs it, the film, its developer and time, and then the enlarger and the paper. Ansel Adams's Zone System described it as exposing for the shadows and developing for the highlights, then printing for the picture.

This mode starts from Kodak Tri-X in D-76, printed on Ilford Multigrade IV RC at grade 2 with a diffusion enlarger. Change the developer and the paper grade together to feel how a negative and a print share the work.
