# Slide: reversal film on a lightbox

Reversal film becomes the picture itself: developed in E-6, it is a positive, seen on a light table or projected. There is no negative, no print and no scanner's correction, so what the film does to color and contrast is what you see.

This mode exposes a slide stock, develops it, and shows it on a D50 lightbox. Slides have little latitude: expose carefully, and expect their saturated, contrasty character.
