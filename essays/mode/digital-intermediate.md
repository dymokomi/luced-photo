# Digital Intermediate

The chain of most films from the early 2000s to the end of film prints: the camera negative is developed and scanned, graded digitally, recorded back to film on a laser recorder, and printed for projection. It combines what only a grade can do, such as turning one hue into another or working in windows, with the look of a projected print.

This mode develops Kodak Vision 500T, scans it, grades it, records it and prints it on Vision 2383. Recording and printing add the print's own contrast and color on top of the grade, as they did then.
