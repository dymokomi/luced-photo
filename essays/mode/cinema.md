# Cinema: negative to print to screen

The classic chain of feature films until the digital intermediate took over in the 2000s. The camera negative was developed, timed with printer lights, printed onto a high-contrast print film and projected in a dark theater.

Here the negative is printed on Kodak Vision 2383 and projected under a xenon arc. The print's own curve and dyes are the tone scale: deep blacks, saturated midtones, highlights that roll off. Printer lights time the color, and the print's silver can be kept, as Technicolor's ENR and Deluxe's CCE did.
