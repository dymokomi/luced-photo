# Black and white

Black-and-white negative, developed in a developer of your choice and scanned. A color filter on the lens decides which colors become light or dark grey, and the developer decides speed, grain, sharpness and how the highlights are held.

This mode puts a Filter stage before the film for that reason, and starts from Fujifilm Acros II in D-76. Try Tri-X in Rodinal for sharp, gritty grain, or a red filter for dark skies.
