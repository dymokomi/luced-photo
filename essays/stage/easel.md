# The easel: framing the picture

In the darkroom, the easel holds the paper under the enlarger and its blades frame the picture. Framing is the last decision, and the photographer's alone.

Here, the easel crops the frame, corrects perspective and rotation (the guides find the lines that should be vertical or horizontal), and darkens or lightens the edges, as printers burned in the corners of a print with their hands or a card to hold the eye in the frame.

Because it comes last, the easel works on the finished picture. Cropping here does not change how the film was exposed or how big its grain is: the grain belongs to the negative and its format, chosen in the Film node.
