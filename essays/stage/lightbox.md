# The lightbox: a slide seen directly

A slide is the picture itself. Reversal film comes out of processing as a positive, so there is nothing to print or invert: it is viewed by light shining through it, on a light table with a loupe, or projected.

Light tables for judging color are made to a standard, D50 light, close to daylight at 5000 K, so that everyone in a print shop or an agency saw the same slide the same way. Seen like this, a slide shows its full range: the deepest dye densities of a good slide reach beyond 3.5, far more than a print on paper can hold.

Here, the slide is lit by D50 light and the light coming through its dyes is what is shown.
