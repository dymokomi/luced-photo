# The grade: the colorist's tools

Once the negative is scanned, it is images again, and a colorist shapes them. On film-originated television this happened on the telecine itself, with controllers such as the da Vinci, adjusting lift, gamma and gain per color and per scene.

The tools here are the familiar ones: tone (contrast, highlights, shadows, whites and blacks), levels, curves, vibrance and saturation, a mixer of eight hue bands, color wheels for shadows, midtones and highlights, and a black-and-white conversion with its own channel weights.

A grade cannot add what the film did not record. It can, however, push a look much further than any lab could, which is why this stage sits after the scan: the film decides the material, the grade decides the picture.
