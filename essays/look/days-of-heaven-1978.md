# Days of Heaven (1978)

Néstor Almendros shot most of it at the magic hour, with no fill light. As the light went he pushed the negative a stop, then pulled the 85 filter to gain nearly a stop more, and finally slowed the camera. Nights were pushed one or two stops. The prints were blown up to 70 mm.

Almendros shot Kodak's 5247, the first ECN-2 negative, which is not here; EXR 200T 5293 stands in. This look pushes it a stop and takes off the 85, so the light falls on the film as it was.
