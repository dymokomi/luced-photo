# The French Dispatch (2021): black and white

About half of The French Dispatch is in black and white, shot on real black-and-white negative, Kodak's Eastman Double-X 5222, not desaturated color. Robert Yeoman had both stocks processed normally, "without any push or pull," in Paris, in Super 35.

This look uses Double-X on Super 35, developed normally and scanned.
