# Munich (2005)

Janusz Kamiński shot Munich in Super 35 on Kodak Vision 500T 5279, EXR 200T 5293 and Fuji negatives, and every release print went through Technicolor's ENR at an infrared level of 65 to 75. ENR adds a black-and-white development bath that leaves silver in the print where it is dense, so blacks turn rich and colors drain. It was not the first film to keep silver: Kamiński had used ENR on Amistad and Saving Private Ryan, and bleach bypass goes back to 1960.

This look prints 5279 on Vision 2383 with its silver kept at an infrared density of 0.70. The Fuji stocks of the Middle East scenes are not modelled.
