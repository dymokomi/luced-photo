# Barry Lyndon (1975)

Kubrick and John Alcott pushed the whole film a stop on 5254, so interiors and candlelit scenes matched. The candlelight was possible only with a Zeiss 50 mm f/0.7 lens built for NASA. On exteriors Alcott never used an 85 filter on the tungsten stock and took out the blue in printing, and a Tiffen Low Contrast No. 3 softened every shot.

This look uses 5254 pushed in ECN-1 (16 minutes, an inference) and prints it balanced, as the timer would have. The Low Contrast filter is not modelled.
