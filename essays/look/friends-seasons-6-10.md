# Friends, seasons 6–10

The later seasons look brighter and cleaner, consistent with Kodak's Vision stocks, which replaced EXR from 1996, and with changes in the show's telecine work.

This look uses Vision 500T 5279 through the television chain, timed warmer and less magenta than the early seasons and with less grain, matched to the broadcast's frames. As for the early seasons, the stock is an inference: no source names it.
