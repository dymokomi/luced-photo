# Lawrence of Arabia (1962)

Freddie Young photographed the desert in Super Panavision 70 on 65 mm Eastman Color negative, for 70 mm prints. The mirage was shot on a Panavision telephoto of over 400 mm. "I just shot outdoors and stopped down," Young said.

The negative was most likely 5250, which 5251 replaced at the same speed in 1962; 5251 stands in. This look exposes it on 65 mm through an 85 filter, as a tungsten stock in daylight needs, develops it in ECN-1, prints and projects it. Period print stock is not modelled: Vision 2383 stands in.
