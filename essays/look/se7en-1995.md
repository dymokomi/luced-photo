# Se7en (1995)

Darius Khondji wanted color noir, blacks as deep as black and white could give. Interiors were shot on EXR 200T 5293 at box speed, in Super 35, nearly wide open. A number of the release prints went through Deluxe's new CCE process, which keeps about three quarters of the print's silver: "to get those beautiful blacks, it would drain out a lot of the color." Color drained in the lab was put back by the art department.

This look prints 5293 with its silver kept at an infrared density of 1.85, CCE's level. The film's other stocks, 5245 for exteriors and 5287 pushed a stop for nights, are not modelled.
