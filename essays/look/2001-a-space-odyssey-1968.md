# 2001: A Space Odyssey (1968)

Kubrick and Geoffrey Unsworth shot 2001 in Super Panavision 70 on 65 mm Eastman Color Negative, very likely 5251; its faster successor 5254 was only presented in May 1968, after the premiere. The 70 mm prints came from MGM's Metrocolor lab; 35 mm prints were Technicolor dye-transfer reductions. The Dawn of Man was shot on a stage with front projection, and the Star Gate with slit-scan.

This look exposes 5251 on 65 mm, develops it in ECN-1, prints it on Vision 2383 standing in for the period print film, and projects it.
