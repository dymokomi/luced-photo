# Saving Private Ryan (1998)

Kamiński shot EXR 200T 5293 pushed a stop to 400, with a half Coral filter where an 85 would normally go, for a slightly blue cast. A Panaflasher flashed the negative about 15 percent, so the blacks would not be sharp and the color would desaturate, and the prints went through ENR, which the lab ran at an infrared level of 1.00, "one of the highest levels of ENR used to date." Panavision stripped the coatings off his lenses for flare and flat contrast, and a 45 or 90 degree shutter froze every grain of sand.

This look sets all of that except the uncoated lenses and the shutter, which are not modelled. The half Coral's strength is an estimate.
