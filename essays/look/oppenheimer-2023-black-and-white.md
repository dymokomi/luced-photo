# Oppenheimer (2023): black and white

For Strauss's timeline Kodak made Double-X 5222 in 65 mm for the first time, prototype cans with handwritten labels. FotoKem had never processed 65 mm black and white, and refilled a 65 mm processor with black-and-white chemistry for it. The cameras needed new gates for the stock's thickness.

This look uses Double-X on 65 mm, developed and scanned. Its rating, developer and print stock were not published.
