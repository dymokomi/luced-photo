# Three Kings (1999): cross-processed

Once the characters leave the base, Sigel shot Ektachrome that Kodak made for the production and cross-processed it in negative chemistry: the crew signed a waiver, a small lab ran it, and "off a half a stop, you were shit out of luck."

This look exposes Ektachrome E100, the nearest Ektachrome here, develops it in C-41 as a negative and prints it: harsh contrast, a clear base with no mask, and loud, shifted color.
