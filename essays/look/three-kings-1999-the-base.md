# Three Kings (1999): the base

For the opening on the American base, Newton Thomas Sigel skip-bleached the camera negative, "not in the print, but in the negative, which is much more radical." David O. Russell's reference was newspaper color photos of the Gulf War, "very contrasty," that "blow out."

The stock was a Kodak daylight negative, most likely Vision 250D 5246; Vision3 250D stands in. This look keeps all the negative's silver and prints it.
