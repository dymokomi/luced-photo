# Nineteen Eighty-Four (1984)

Roger Deakins had the bleach bypassed on all the prints, at Kays Laboratory in London, rather than on the camera negative. Earlier home releases did not show it; Deakins has said only the recent Criterion release reflects the intent. Darius Khondji named it as an inspiration for Delicatessen.

This look prints with all the silver kept, an infrared density of 2.40. The camera negative is not documented: EXR 200T stands in.
