# Oppenheimer (2023): color

Hoyte van Hoytema shot on 65 mm, IMAX 15-perforation and Panavision System 65: Vision3 250D for exteriors and bright interiors, 500T for low light. The color scenes are Oppenheimer's own point of view. FotoKem printed everything, and IMAX 70 mm, 70 mm and 35 mm film prints went out, with a digital master matched to them.

This look uses Vision3 250D on 65 mm, printed and projected. The IMAX frame, larger still, is not modelled.
