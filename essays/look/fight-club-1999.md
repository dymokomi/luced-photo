# Fight Club (1999)

Jeff Cronenweth rated his stocks at box speed and exposed generously, so the negatives were dense and printed down. Vision 500T 5279 carried the interiors and nights, some night exteriors were flashed about five percent in the lab, and a number of prints went through Technicolor's ENR at an infrared level of 0.80.

This look prints 5279 from Super 35 with silver kept at 0.80. The daylight stocks, EXR 5248 and Vision 250D 5246, are not modelled; for the flashed night exteriors, add 5 percent Flashing on the Film stage.
