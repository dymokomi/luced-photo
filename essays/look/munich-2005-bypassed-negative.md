# Munich (2005): the bypassed scenes

For the semi-documentary scenes, the refugee camps and the Munich assault, the bleach was skipped on the camera negative itself, at Technicolor in Los Angeles and London and at Éclair in Paris. Silver left on a negative sits in the scene's highlights, so whites blow out. To tame the contrast the negative was pulled one and a half stops: "It's almost like shooting color reversal," Kamiński said. The prints then also had ENR, so the film stacks both.

This look keeps all the negative's silver, develops it for about 2:10 instead of 3:00 (Kodak tables a one-stop pull at 2:30; the extra half stop is extrapolated), and prints with ENR at 0.70.
