# Friends, seasons 1–5

Friends was shot on 35 mm Panavision negative under the bright, warm light of a multi-camera sitcom stage, developed and transferred on a telecine to standard-definition tape, never printed. No source names its stocks; a fast tungsten Kodak negative of the EXR line is the likely choice for the early years.

This look uses EXR 500T 5298 through the television chain, its telecine timing warm and a little magenta, flatter and grainier, matched to the cast of the broadcast's own frames.
