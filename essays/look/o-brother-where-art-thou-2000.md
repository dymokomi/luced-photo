# O Brother, Where Art Thou? (2000)

The Coens and Roger Deakins wanted the faded look of hand-tinted postcards: dry, golden fields. Bleach bypass and Deluxe's ACE were tested and rejected because they drain all colors alike. Instead the whole film was scanned at 2K, and for ten weeks colorist Julius Friede at Cinesite turned the greens of grass and trees toward dry yellows and browns, then recorded it back to film. It was the first feature finished entirely through a digital intermediate.

This look runs Vision 500T 5279 from Super 35 through the scan and grade, turning greens toward yellow and lowering their saturation. The amounts are an inference from the accounts.
