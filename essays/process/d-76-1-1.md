# D-76 1:1

D-76 diluted with an equal part of water and thrown away after one use. Diluted, the sulfite that dissolves grain edges is weaker and the developer runs out sooner in dense areas, so Kodak notes "greater sharpness" and a "slight increase in graininess" against the stock solution.

Development takes longer, about ten and a half minutes for Acros II at 20 °C, and is less sensitive to temperature: Kodak's tables for diluted developers change about 5.5 % per degree, against 8 to 9 % for stock solutions. Many photographers use it as their everyday developer for its sharpness and its fresh, consistent results.
