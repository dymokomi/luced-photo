# Rodinal 1+25

Rodinal is one of the oldest photographic products still made: Momme Andresen patented it for Agfa in 1891. It is a concentrate of p-aminophenol in strong alkali with almost no sulfite, diluted just before use.

With little sulfite there is no solvent action on the grain. Each grain develops whole and sharp-edged, so the grain is crisp and clearly defined, and Agfa sold it for its "very good contour sharpness". It costs a little speed, about a third of a stop. Its look is the classic one of gritty, sharp black-and-white: every grain visible, every edge clear.
