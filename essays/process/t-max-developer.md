# Kodak T-MAX developer

A liquid developer Kodak made for its T-MAX films, whose flat, tabular "T-grain" crystals capture light efficiently but need a developer that brings out their full speed. Kodak describes it as giving enhanced shadow detail, at normal ratings and when pushing.

It is used at 1+4 and gives roughly a third of a stop more shadow speed than D-76, with a little more grain. T-MAX P3200, a film meant to be pushed, was usually developed in it.
