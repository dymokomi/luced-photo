# Rodinal 1+100, stand development

A very dilute developer left almost untouched: the film is agitated at the start and then stands for an hour. Where the image is dense, the developer around it is exhausted within minutes and development stops; in the shadows it continues. The result is strong compensation, highlights held back and shadows built up, and strong edge effects as exhausted developer and released bromide spread around dense areas.

Agfa's own figures for 1+100 show real speed loss, Tri-X at about EI 250. Stand development has risks: bromide-laden developer can stream down from dense areas, leaving streaks known as bromide drag. Photographers use it for scenes of extreme contrast and for its etched, glowing edges.
