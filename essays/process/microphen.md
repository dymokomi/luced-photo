# Ilford Microphen

Microphen is Ilford's speed-increasing developer: phenidone and hydroquinone, buffered with borax at low alkalinity. Phenidone, discovered at Ilford by J. D. Kendall in 1940, is barely held back by the bromide that development releases, so development keeps going in the deepest shadows.

Ilford claims up to half a stop more speed than ID-11 with comparable grain, a high "speed to grain ratio". It is the developer Ilford recommends for pushing HP5 Plus, with times given up to EI 3200.
