# Ilford Perceptol

Perceptol is a fine-grain developer of the solvent type, like Kodak's Microdol-X: metol with a large amount of sulfite and some chloride, at a nearly neutral pH. It dissolves a good part of each grain as it develops, and that silver is redeposited as fine particles, so the image is made of smaller specks.

The price is speed. Ilford rates HP5 Plus at EI 250 and Tri-X at EI 200 in Perceptol, against 400 in ID-11. Photographers use it when the finest grain matters more than speed: large prints from 35 mm, smooth skies, portraits.
