# Kodak HC-110, dilution B

HC-110 is a syrupy concentrate, diluted just before use; dilution B (1+31) is the one Kodak's times are given for. Its developing agents sit in an organic solvent rather than water, so the concentrate keeps for years, which made it the developer of schools, newspapers and photographers who develop only now and then.

Development is quick, under four minutes for Tri-X at 20 °C, and gives full speed with a slightly coarser grain than D-76 and good sharpness. It is also more sensitive to temperature than most, close to ten percent per degree.
