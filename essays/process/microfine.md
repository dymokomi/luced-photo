# Fujifilm Microfine

Fujifilm's own fine-grain developer, and the one its Acros data sheet measures granularity in: an RMS granularity of 7 for Acros II, among the finest figures of any black-and-white film. Unlike most fine-grain developers it costs Acros no speed: ten minutes at 20 °C at EI 100.

Fujifilm's table for it runs from twelve and a half minutes at 18 °C to under six at 26 °C, about ten percent per degree.
