# Rodinal 1+50

Rodinal diluted twice as far. The weaker developer exhausts quickly in dense areas: highlights are held back while shadows keep developing, which compresses a contrasty scene, and fresh developer diffusing into a thin area beside a dense one makes edges stand out, the Eberhard effect.

Development takes about twelve minutes for Tri-X at 20 °C, rated around EI 400 at best. It is a favourite for its sharpness and tonality, with grain as defined as at 1+25.
