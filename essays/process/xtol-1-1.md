# Kodak XTOL 1:1

XTOL diluted with an equal part of water and used once. Kodak's sheet promises "slightly greater film speed" and "enhanced sharpness" than the stock solution, with slightly more grain: the usual trade of dilution, where a weaker developer exhausts locally at edges and brings out fine detail.

Like other dilutions it is less sensitive to temperature, about 5.5 % per degree, which makes it forgiving in a home darkroom.
