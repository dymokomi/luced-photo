# Kodak Supra Endura

Supra Endura was the companion to Portra Endura in Kodak Professional's Endura color papers for Process RA-4, with slightly higher contrast and color saturation. Kodak's sheet E-4021 (September 2009) suggests it for portrait, candid, event, high-key and commercial work, and announces that its roll formats would end in 2009, with Supra Endura VC Digital Paper as the replacement.

It shares Portra Endura's construction, spectral sensitivities and dyes: blue-, green- and red-sensitive layers forming yellow, magenta and cyan dyes, with the same dyes whether exposed digitally or through an enlarger. Kodak described brighter blues, cyans, purples and reds, and a "dual layer coupler blending" technology for image quality.

Its Status A characteristic curves, read here from the sheet's vector graphs, are steeper in the mid-tones than Portra Endura's and reach a D-max of about 2.65 in red, 2.59 in green and 2.52 in blue. The sheet's log exposure ticks are unevenly drawn on this graph, so the graph's frame sets the axis; its speed point sits about 0.3 log units to the right of Portra Endura's.
