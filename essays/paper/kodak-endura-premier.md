# Kodak Endura Premier

Endura Premier is a Kodak Professional color paper for Process RA-4, introduced with data sheet E-4070 in October 2011. It is a fast, resin-coated multilayer paper for prints from digital files and from color negatives, made for both portrait and commercial work, and it followed the earlier Endura papers (Portra, Supra and Ultra Endura).

A color negative paper works like a color negative film turned around: three emulsion layers, sensitive to blue, green and red, form yellow, magenta and cyan dyes in RA-4 development, so a print from an orange-masked negative comes out positive. The paper's sensitivities sit in narrow bands that suit the dyes of color negatives and the lasers and LEDs of digital printers, and its safelight is nearly useless: Kodak advises total darkness. Endura Premier's sheet claims a larger color gamut, natural skin tones, cleaner whites (lower D-min) and a higher D-max than the papers before it.

Its curves are read from the sheet's vector graphs: the three Status A characteristic curves for a 0.5 second exposure, reaching D-max of about 2.76 in red, 2.52 in green and 2.44 in blue from a D-min near 0.1; the three layers' spectral sensitivities; and the cyan, magenta and yellow dye densities, which Kodak draws normalized to a peak of 1.0. The sheet gives no ISO speed or range for a color paper.
