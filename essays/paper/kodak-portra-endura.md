# Kodak Portra Endura

Portra Endura was Kodak Professional's low-contrast color paper for Process RA-4, made for portrait, studio and wedding photography. It replaced Portra III paper and was itself discontinued in late 2006, as Kodak's sheet E-4021 (September 2009) records. It was meant to pair with the Portra films, keeping skin tones soft and smooth from highlights to shadows.

Like other RA-4 papers it has three layers sensitive to blue, green and red light that form yellow, magenta and cyan dyes. The Endura generation used the same dyes for digital and optical printing and a new emulsion for both kinds of exposure, and Kodak claimed over 100 years before noticeable fading on home display and over 200 years in dark storage.

The curves here are read from E-4021's vector graphs: the Status A characteristic curves for a 0.5 second exposure, with a long, gentle shoulder that reaches about 2.66 in red and green and 2.43 in blue, and the spectral sensitivities and dye densities the sheet shares with Supra Endura. It is the softest color paper in this set.
