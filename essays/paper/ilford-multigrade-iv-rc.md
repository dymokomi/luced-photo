# Ilford Multigrade IV RC

Multigrade IV RC is Ilford's resin-coated variable-contrast black-and-white enlarging paper of the 1990s and 2000s, the fourth generation of a paper line Ilford began in 1940 and has since carried on with Multigrade RC V. The fact sheet used here (December 2001) lists a 190 g/m² DeLuxe base in glossy, satin and pearl surfaces and a heavier 250 g/m² Portfolio base, and describes the image color as cool to neutral.

A variable-contrast paper replaces a box of fixed grades with one paper and a set of filters. Its emulsion is a blend: a high-contrast component sensitive only to blue light and a low-contrast component also sensitized to green. A yellow filter holds back blue, so the soft component does most of the work and the print is low in contrast; a magenta filter holds back green, so the hard component dominates. Ilford's twelve Multigrade filters run from 00 to 5 in half steps. The sheet gives ISO ranges of R180 for filter 00 down to R40 for filter 5 (R110 unfiltered), and ISO speeds of P200 through filter 3 1/2 and P100 for 4 to 5, so the harder filters need twice the exposure. Its spectral sensitivity graph shows the two parts: a broad blue response and a second peak near 515 nm.

The characteristic curves here are read from the sheet's vector graphs, one per filter from 00 to 5, developed in Multigrade developer 1+9 for one minute at 20 C. Ilford draws them on a relative log exposure axis and as density above the base, reaching a maximum of about 2.05; the ranges measured from the traced curves agree with the sheet's ISO figures to within about 15.
