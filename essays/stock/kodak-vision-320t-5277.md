# Kodak Vision 320T 5277

Vision 320T was made for television. Kodak designed it with a lower contrast than its feature stocks, because a telecine transfer and a television screen both add contrast of their own; starting flatter keeps detail in the faces and in the shadows of a set.

Its 320 speed sat between the 200 and 500 stocks, fast enough for a sitcom's lighting with good depth of field across a stage. Shows such as Everybody Loves Raymond used it. Its look is clean, slightly soft in contrast, and it takes a grade well.
