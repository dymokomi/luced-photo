# Fujifilm Velvia 100

Fujifilm introduced Velvia 100 in 2005, when the original Velvia was withdrawn, as an ISO 100 film with Velvia's saturation. Fujifilm called its color saturation the world's highest, and gave it a diffuse rms granularity of 8, finer than Velvia 50's 9. It uses a color-extension layer adjusted from Velvia 100F's to render sunrise and sunset hues. Velvia 50 returned in 2007, and the two were sold side by side.

Like Velvia 50 it is a reversal film, developed in E-6 into a slide, with deep blacks, saturated greens and reds and little room for overexposure. Its resolving power is 160 lines/mm at high contrast, among the highest of any slide film. Fujifilm withdrew it from the United States in 2021 over a trace chemical restricted by the EPA.
