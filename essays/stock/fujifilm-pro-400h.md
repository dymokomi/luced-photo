# Fujifilm Pro 400H

Pro 400H was Fujifilm's professional ISO 400 daylight color negative, processed in C-41 (Fujifilm's CN-16). Fujifilm's sheet describes it as reproducing neutral greys faithfully from under- to overexposure and giving superb skin tones with smooth gradation from highlights to shadows, which made it a portrait and wedding film. Fujifilm discontinued it in 2021.

Its distinctive feature was a fourth color-sensitive layer, in addition to the usual blue, green and red, meant to make the film see colors more as the eye does. The sheet draws it as a "cyan sensitive layer" peaking near 520 nm, about 0.9 log units below the green layer's peak. It forms no image of its own: through interlayer effects it adjusts the red layer's response to blue-green light.

The sheet's curves are vectors and are read here directly, the fourth layer included. Fujifilm gives a diffuse rms granularity of 4, but on its own negative-film scale, which it says cannot be compared with slide films; the grain strength used here is an estimate matched to Kodak Portra 400.
