# Kodak EXR 200T 5293

The medium-speed tungsten stock of the EXR line, rated EI 200 at 3200 K. With less speed it has finer grain than 500T, and it was a workhorse of studio productions in the early and mid 1990s, both for features and for television.

Directors of photography on television valued its slightly higher contrast: material that is going to be transferred, compressed and broadcast tends to lose snap along the way, so a negative that starts a little punchier survives better. The DP of Spin City chose it for exactly that reason.

The data sheet for 5293 also publishes the spectral densities of its cyan, magenta and yellow dyes. They are the dyes of the ECN-2 process, which this simulator uses for its EXR stocks.
