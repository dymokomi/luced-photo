# Kodak Vision3 250D 5207

Vision3 250D is the medium-speed daylight stock of the family, introduced in 2009. It is rated EI 250 at 5500 K and 64 under tungsten with an 80A filter, and it is developed in ECN-2 like the other Vision3 negatives.

Kodak describes it as Vision3 technology in a daylight emulsion: the look of Vision2, with less grain in the shadows and more than two stops of extra highlight latitude. The sheet singles out the red and green records, whose grain improves most when the film is underexposed, so dark parts of a scan come out cleaner.

For an exterior in sun, 250D gives enough speed to stop down for depth of field while keeping the grain of a medium-speed film. Its MTF curves are among the best of the Vision3 negatives, holding above 40 percent at 70 cycles per millimetre in the blue and green records.
