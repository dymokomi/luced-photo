# Ilford HP5 Plus

HP5 Plus is Ilford's fast black-and-white film, rated ISO 400. Its line goes back to Ilford's HP (hypersensitive panchromatic) films of the 1930s; HP5 appeared in 1976 and the current HP5 Plus in 1989. Like Tri-X it uses conventional, cubic grain, and the two have long been the standard fast black-and-white films.

Ilford's sheet recommends it at EI 400 but says it produces good prints at up to EI 3200 with extended development, and notes that its exposure range is based on practical tests, not on the foot speed of the ISO standard. It is known for wide latitude and an easy, forgiving tonal range.

Ilford publishes its graphs as images, with one characteristic curve, in ILFOTEC HC for 6 1/2 minutes at 20 C, on a relative exposure scale, and a wedge spectrogram to tungsten light reaching to about 655 nm. Both are traced here. Ilford gives no MTF or rms granularity, so this stock's sharpness and grain are less certain than those of Kodak's films.
