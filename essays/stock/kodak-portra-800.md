# Kodak Portra 800

Portra 800 is the fastest film in Kodak's Portra line of professional color negatives for Process C-41. Kodak's sheet describes it as having well balanced saturation, very fine grain for its speed and "best-in-class underexposure latitude", and recommends it for long lenses and low light.

It is the only current Kodak still film whose sheet draws push-processed curves: besides normal processing at EI 800, it shows the film exposed at EI 1600 and given a one-stop push, and at EI 3200 with a two-stop push. Pushing raises contrast and density across the scale; at the same exposure the green layer reaches about 1.55 after one stop of push and 1.7 after two, against 1.4 normally. The simulator keeps all three sets of curves.

Its Print Grain Index is 48 for a 4x6 print from 35 mm, against Portra 400's 37. Kodak gives no rms granularity, so the grain strength here is an estimate scaled from that index. The sheet puts normal exposure at log H -1.74.
