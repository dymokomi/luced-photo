# Kodak Vision3 50D 5203

The slowest Vision3 negative, 50D was added in 2011 and Kodak said its grain was the world's finest. It is rated EI 50 in daylight and 12 under tungsten with an 80A filter, and it is processed in ECN-2.

Kodak aimed it at bright, high-contrast daylight exteriors, where its long exposure range holds both a sunlit sky and a face in shade, and at film-out work, where an image recorded from digital files needs the finest grain available. Its granularity curves are the lowest of the family: in the red and green records the rms granularity stays near or below 0.006 across the whole scale.

Like 500T, it has a respooled cousin for still cameras: CineStill 50D is 5203 with the rem-jet backing removed so that it can be developed in C-41.
