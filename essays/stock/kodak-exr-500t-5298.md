# Kodak EXR 500T 5298

EXR was Kodak's motion picture negative line of the late 1980s and early 1990s. Its 500T was the fast, tungsten-balanced member, rated EI 500 under 3200 K studio light and EI 320 in daylight behind an 85 filter. Kodak's 1993 data sheet for 5298 is the source of the curves used here.

It is a stock of its time. Its red layer is noticeably softer than its green and blue, its blue layer the grainiest, and it carries a rem-jet backing, a black layer on the back that stops light reflecting off the base, so it shows little halation.

Fast tungsten negative like this was the natural choice for 1990s American television shot on 35 mm, such as Friends: sets lit by tungsten lamps, a negative telecined straight to video, never printed. No source names the stock Friends used in each season; 5298 and its slower sibling 5293 are the likely candidates for the early years.
