# Kodak Vision3 200T 5213

Vision3 200T came out in 2010 as the medium-speed tungsten stock of the family. Kodak described it as a 200-speed film with the image structure of a 100-speed one, two films in one. It is rated EI 200 at 3200 K and 125 in daylight with an 85 filter, and it is processed in ECN-2.

It shares the Vision3 traits: extended highlight latitude, reduced grain in the shadows, and a look matched to the other stocks, so that scenes shot on 200T cut with scenes shot on 500T or 250D. Kodak recommends it both for controlled studio interiors and for high-contrast exteriors.

Its sheet marks normal exposure at log H -1.284, the centre of a sixteen-stop scale; there the green record reads about 1.35 in Status M density, close to where the other Vision3 negatives place a grey card.
