# Eastman Double-X 5222

Double-X is Kodak's black-and-white camera negative for motion pictures, usually dated to 1959 and still made today in 35 mm (5222) and 16 mm (7222). It is a fast panchromatic film, rated EI 250 in daylight and 200 under tungsten for development to a gamma of 0.65.

Laboratories develop it in D-96, a developer made for motion picture black-and-white work, and the development time sets the contrast. Kodak's 2022 sheet shows the range: 4 minutes gives a gamma of 0.50 and 12 minutes 1.05, with 6½ minutes landing at the recommended 0.66. Its diffuse rms granularity is 14, and its resolving power is 100 lines per millimetre at high contrast.

Still photographers know it as CineStill bwXX, which is Double-X respooled for 35 mm cameras.
